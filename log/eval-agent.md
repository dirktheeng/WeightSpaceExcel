- EXP and LN: new network ev_pow2 (id 99), one forward pass of 302 values. Design reasoning: NORM2 merges the down and up exponent cascades of primitives.md NORM into 11 two-sided stages (512, 512, 256, ..., 1; only one side can fire for a given x, so one multiply per stage) giving a = m 2^e with m in [0.5, 2], exact. Scale factors are built as 2^-c gd + 2^c gu + (1 - gd - gu) in one row each, because the obvious 1 + (2^-c - 1) g form rounds 2^-512 - 1 to -1 and would give 0. Fold m into [sqrt(1/2), sqrt(2)]; t = m - 1 exact (Sterbenz); s = t / (m + 1) by a chord start (|1 - d y0| <= 0.031, worked out at d = 2.0607) and 4 Newton steps (error 0.031 -> 9e-4 -> 9e-7 -> 7e-13 -> 5e-25); ln m = 2 s (1 + s2/3 + ... + s2^10/21), truncation below 1e-18 since s2 <= 0.0295. L = e ln2_hi (exact, fdlibm split) + (2 P s + e ln2_lo).
- y = b L guarded: ys = (b 2^-512) L cannot overflow; |ys| > 1e-151 (|y| above about 1341) means overflow (#NUM!) or underflow (0); otherwise y = (b ok) L is finite. This keeps V1 for b up to 1e308 without SAFE_MUL.
- EXP: k = round(y / ln2) with the 1.5 2^52 constant; r = (y - k ln2_hi) - k ln2_lo; exp(r) by Taylor degree 14 (|r| <= 0.347, truncation 4e-18). Overflow is decided exactly before scaling: Q 2^k >= 2^1024 iff k >= 1025 or (k = 1024 and Q >= 1), because Q is in [0.707, 1.415]; then 11 exact power-of-two stages scale by 2^k (k zeroed on overflow so no infinity is ever formed).
