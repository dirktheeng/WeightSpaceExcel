# Weight block notes: weights/ui/fnum.wt (id 131, General number to text)

Hand-written. The digit chains are the DIGITS block of weights/prim/digits.md, retyped and renumbered (chains k = 1..12). Log: log/ui-agent.md; decision U5.
**Computes.** Writes the General display text of x = A.PAY at P0 + i (T0 = i, starting at 0), one character per step, then a 0, and returns to (LR, LP) with P0 at the terminator. Rule: at most 11 characters, counting the sign and the point. k = max(0, 10 - integer digits - sign) decimals, N = ROUND(|x| 10^k), and trailing fraction zeros are stripped along with the point if nothing remains (1/3 gives 0.333333333, -1/3 gives -0.33333333, 123456.123456 gives 123456.1235). Every step recomputes everything from x and selects character i, so the network keeps no state but i.
**Derivation.** Integer digits come from 10 exact compares a' < 10^j. 10^k is selected exactly by one-hot. N = ROUND(a' 10^k) is one IEEE multiply then ROUND (R5: a halfway case can differ from Excel in the last shown digit). f_k = floor(N/10^k) by DIGITS; d_j = f_j - 10 f_(j+1); integer length L = 1 + relu(nd - k - 1). Trailing zeros: P_t = [d_0..d_t all 0], tz = sum of (P_t AND k >= t + 1). Character i: sign if i < s, digit j = k + L - 1 - (i - s) + isFrac for integer and fraction positions, '.' at i - s = L when a fraction is shown, terminator at i = T.
**Limits.** |x| >= 1e11 shows its integer digits instead of Excel's 1.23457E+11 (up to 12 digits are correct; above that wrong). Nonzero |x| < 5e-10 shows 0 (or -0) instead of scientific notation. The text is not shortened to fit narrow columns.
**Parameters.** about 1,600. **Steps.** characters + 1. **Multi-role weights.** None. **Tests.** test_fnum_general (13 cases).
